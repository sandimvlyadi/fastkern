# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Fastkern community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members are expected to interact professionally and respectfully regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

We commit to fostering an environment where people can:

- Participate without harassment or discrimination.
- Share different perspectives respectfully.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Ask questions without fear of ridicule.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on improving the project and its community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Demonstrating empathy toward other community members.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.
- Providing constructive and actionable feedback.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Focusing discussions on technical and community outcomes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Threats or targeted abuse.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and community activities, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- Discussions and code reviews.
- Project documentation.
- Official project communication channels.
- Public or private interactions when someone is officially representing the project.

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when behavior outside project spaces materially affects the safety or wellbeing of the project community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate these standards.

Enforcement decisions should be fair, proportionate, and focused on maintaining a safe and productive community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the project maintainers through the repository's configured private communication channel.

Please include:

- A description of what happened.
- When and where it occurred.
- The people involved, if known.
- Any relevant supporting information.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on the circumstances and severity of a violation, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions:

### 1. Correction

A private or public clarification when behavior is inappropriate but does not require a stronger response.

### 2. Warning

A formal warning describing the violation and the expected behavior going forward.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

Temporary restriction from participating in project spaces or community interactions.

### 4. Permanent Restriction

Permanent removal from project community spaces for serious or repeated violations.

The response will take into account the nature, severity, frequency, and impact of the behavior.
